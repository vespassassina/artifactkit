How is margin calculated?

Gross margin before central allocation. Intercompany eliminated at source. Partial periods excluded.

Why is Healthcare margin blank?

The source system returned no value for the period. It is shown as – rather than estimated or carried forward.

What counts as an active account?

Any account with at least one invoiced transaction in the period.
