How is contribution defined?

Revenue less directly attributable operating cost. Central overhead is not allocated, so regional figures are not comparable to statutory segment results.

Why is Nordics margin blank?

The cost feed did not deliver for July and August. Rather than estimate, the cell is blank and the region is excluded from the margin column of the total.
