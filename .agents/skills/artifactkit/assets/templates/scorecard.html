03

Rubric

A scorecard without its rubric is an opinion in a grid. You will be challenged on these definitions, so they are stated.

Delivery — 30%

5 — on time and in full, every order, no expediting. 4 — one late line, self-reported before the due date. 3 — occasional slippage inside the tolerance window. 2 — repeated slippage requiring expediting cost. 1 — missed commitments causing downstream stoppage.

Quality — 25%

Defect rate against the agreed AQL, plus whether non-conformances are reported by the supplier or discovered by us. Self-reporting is scored higher than a clean record found by inspection.

Cost — 20%

Total landed cost against benchmark, not unit price. A cheaper unit that arrives late scores worse than a dearer one that does not.

Responsiveness — 15%

Time to a substantive answer on a technical query. 5 — same working day. 3 — within three days. 1 — needs chasing. Measured from our ticket log, not from supplier self-assessment.

Compliance — 10%

Certification currency, audit findings closed within the agreed window, and subcontractor disclosure. Binary failures here override the weighted score entirely — see the standing column.

How disagreement is handled

Where reviewer spread exceeds 1.5 points the cell is marked SPREAD and the panel re-scores together. Averaging a genuine disagreement hides it.
